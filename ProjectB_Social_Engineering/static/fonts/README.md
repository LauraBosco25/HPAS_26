Place the Polimi icon font files here (copied from the Politecnico di Milano webcommons or your project's assets).

Expected filenames (place all of them):
- polimi_icons_line.eot
- polimi_icons_line.ttf
- polimi_icons_line.woff
- polimi_icons_line.svg
- polimi_icons_solid.eot
- polimi_icons_solid.ttf
- polimi_icons_solid.woff
- polimi_icons_solid.svg

Once the files are in `static/fonts/`, the app will serve them at `/static/fonts/<filename>` and `static/ateneo.css.css` is already configured to load them from there.

If you want, upload the files here and I will place them for you and run a smoke test.
